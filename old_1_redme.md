## Current Research Direction

### Autonomous Agricultural Systems

My primary ongoing research direction is **AI-enabled autonomous agricultural
machines**.

My work on **APS Flow** explores an autonomous agricultural vehicle capable of
perceiving crop environments and supporting precision pesticide spraying.

The current research pipeline includes:

```text
Agricultural Data
       ↓
Image Annotation
       ↓
Preprocessing & Augmentation
       ↓
YOLO-based Detection
       ↓
Plant / Fruit Perception
       ↓
Crop-row Understanding
       ↓
Real-time Environment Understanding
       ↓
Autonomous Navigation
```

The broader objective is to investigate how computer vision, AI, edge computing,
and robotics can be combined to operate reliably in dynamic agricultural
environments.
